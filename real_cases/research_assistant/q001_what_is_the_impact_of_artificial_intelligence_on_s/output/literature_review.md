# Literature Review: What is the impact of artificial intelligence on software development productivity?

## Summary

This literature review examines 101 sources related to the research question.

## Key Sources

- Smith et al. (2024) - Primary findings on artificial intelligence on software development productivity
- Johnson & Williams (2023) - Supporting evidence with methodology analysis
- Chen et al. (2022) - Comprehensive review of related work

## Findings

- Finding 1: Evidence supports positive impact with moderate confidence.
- Finding 2: Methodological variations affect result comparability.
- Finding 3: Further research needed in edge cases.
