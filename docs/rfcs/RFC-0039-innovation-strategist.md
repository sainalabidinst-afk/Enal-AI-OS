## Motivasi

Organizations must anticipate and respond to emerging trends to maintain competitive advantage. Leaders need systematic approaches to:

1. **Trend Analysis** — identify and evaluate emerging technology and market trends
2. **Technology Forecast** — predict adoption curves and time-to-mainstream
3. **Competitive Intelligence** — analyze competitor positioning and threats
4. **Scenario Planning** — develop strategic scenarios for different futures

Capability Pack Innovation Strategist provides technology foresight and strategic planning capabilities for informed decision-making.

---

## Tujuan

1. **Trend Analysis** — analyze technology and market trends across categories with impact and timeframe classification
2. **Technology Forecast** — Gartner-style Hype Cycle adoption curves with peak/mainstream/plateau year projections
3. **Competitive Intelligence** — competitor strength/weakness analysis with threat level and opportunity identification
4. **Scenario Planning** — generate strategic scenarios with likelihood, probability, and recommended actions
5. **Opportunity Identification** — identify high-impact innovation opportunities from trend signals
